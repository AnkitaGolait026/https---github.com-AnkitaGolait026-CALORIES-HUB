🍲 Poha (Flattened Rice)

🧾 Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 medium onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 green chili (finely chopped)
  • 1/4 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Fresh coriander leaves (for garnish)
  • 1/2 lemon (for juice)

👩‍🍳 Steps:

  1. Rinse Poha: Wash poha in a colander under running water for 10–15 seconds. Let it sit and soften for 5 minutes.
  2. Temper: Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter. Then add peanuts and roast until golden.
  3. Add chopped onions, green chili, and curry leaves. Sauté until onions turn translucent.
  4. Add turmeric powder and mix well.
  5. Add soaked poha, salt, and sugar (if using). Mix gently without breaking poha.
  6. Cover and steam for 2–3 minutes on low flame.
  7. Turn off heat, squeeze lemon juice, and mix.
  8. Serve: Garnish with coriander leaves and optionally sev or grated coconut. Serve hot!
