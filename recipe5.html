🍢 Grilled Tofu Skewers

🧾 Ingredients:

  • 200g firm tofu (cut into 1-inch cubes)
  • 1 bell pepper (cut into chunks)
  • 1 onion (cut into chunks)
  • 1 zucchini (sliced thick)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp paprika or chili flakes
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Wooden or metal skewers

👩‍🍳 Steps:

  1. Prepare Marinade: In a bowl, mix olive oil, soy sauce, lemon juice, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Add tofu cubes and veggies to the marinade. Toss gently and let sit for 15–30 minutes.
  3. Skewer: Thread tofu and veggies alternately on skewers.
  4. Grill: Grill on a hot pan or BBQ for 3–4 mins each side until golden and slightly charred.
  5. Serve: Serve hot with mint chutney, hummus, or your favorite dip.
