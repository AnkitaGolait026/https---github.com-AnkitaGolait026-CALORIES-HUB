🥗 Vegan Chickpea Salad

🧾 Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups cooked chickpeas (or 1 can), rinsed
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2–3 tbsp chopped cilantro or parsley
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: 1/2 tsp cumin or chaat masala
  • Optional: 1 chopped avocado

👩‍🍳 Steps:

  1. Combine Veggies: Mix chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, onion, bell pepper, carrot, and herbs in a bowl.
  2. Make Dressing: Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, salt, pepper, and optional spices in a small bowl.
  3. Mix: Pour dressing over the salad and toss everything well.
  4. Let it Sit: Rest for 5–10 mins to absorb flavors.
  5. Serve: Enjoy fresh or chill for a while if desired.
