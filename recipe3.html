🍛 Paneer Butter Masala

🧾 Ingredients:

For the Gravy:

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 bay leaf (tej patta)
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • 2 green cardamoms
  • 1 medium onion (chopped)
  • 2-3 garlic cloves (chopped)
  • 1-inch ginger (chopped)
  • 3-4 medium tomatoes (chopped)
  • 10-15 cashews
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Water (as needed)

For Finishing:

  • 200-250g paneer (cubed)
  • 1 tbsp fresh cream
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (crushed)
  • Fresh coriander leaves (for garnish)

👩‍🍳 Steps:

  1. Make the Gravy Base: Heat butter and oil in a pan. Add bay leaf, cloves, cinnamon, and cardamom. Sauté briefly.
  2. Add onions, garlic, and ginger. Cook till golden brown.
  3. Add tomatoes and cashews. Cook till soft.
  4. Add chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
  5. Let it cool slightly, then blend into a smooth paste.
  6. Cook the Gravy: In the same pan, add butter and the blended paste. Cook for 5–7 minutes.
  7. Add Paneer: Put in paneer cubes and simmer for 3–4 minutes.
  8. Finish: Add cream, garam masala, and crushed kasuri methi. Mix gently.
  9. Serve: Garnish with coriander and serve hot with naan or rice.
