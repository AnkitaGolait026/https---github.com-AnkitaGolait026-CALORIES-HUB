🥣 Oats & Fruits Bowl

🧾 Ingredients:

  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup milk (or almond/soy milk for vegan option)
  • 1 tsp honey or maple syrup (optional)
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon powder (optional)
  • Chopped fruits (banana, apple, berries, mango)
  • 1 tbsp nuts/seeds (almonds, walnuts, chia seeds, flax seeds)
  • A few raisins or dates (optional)

👩‍🍳 Steps:

  1. Cook the Oats: In a pan, add oats and milk. Cook on medium heat for 4–5 mins, stirring until creamy.
  2. Add cinnamon powder if desired.
  3. Sweeten (Optional): Add honey/maple syrup after turning off the heat. Mix well.
  4. Assemble the Bowl: Pour the oats into a bowl and top with chopped fruits.
  5. Sprinkle with nuts, seeds, raisins, or dates.
  6. Serve: Enjoy warm or chill for 10 minutes if preferred cold.
