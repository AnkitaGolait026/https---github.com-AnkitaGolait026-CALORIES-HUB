🍛 Rajma Chawal

🧾 Ingredients:

For Rajma (Kidney Beans Curry):

  • 1 cup rajma (kidney beans), soaked overnight
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 large onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves (for garnish)

For Chawal (Rice):

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp salt

👩‍🍳 Steps:

  1. Cook Rajma: Pressure cook soaked rajma with 3 cups water and salt for 4–5 whistles until soft.
  2. Make the Curry: Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds and bay leaf. Then add onions and sauté till golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook till oil separates.
  5. Add turmeric, chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook for 2 mins.
  6. Add boiled rajma along with its water. Simmer for 10–15 minutes until thick.
  7. Sprinkle garam masala and garnish with coriander leaves.
  8. Cook Rice: Wash and boil rice with water and salt until soft. Drain excess water.
  9. Serve: Serve hot rajma with steamed rice for a comforting meal.
